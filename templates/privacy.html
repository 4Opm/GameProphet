Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

GameProphet is a football match prediction simulation for entertainment purposes only. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

GameProphet does not require registration or login. We do not collect any personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses or payment details.

The only data stored in our database relates to football matches and AI agent predictions — no user data is collected or stored.

Cookies

GameProphet does not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. We use Google Analytics which may set cookies to collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited and time spent on the site. No personally identifiable information is collected through cookies.

Third party services

GameProphet uses the following third party services:

  • football-data.org — provides football match data
  • The Odds API — provides bookmaker odds data
  • Groq — provides AI inference for agent predictions
  • Supabase — provides database hosting
  • Railway — provides application hosting
  • Google Analytics — used to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google may collect anonymized usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Each of these services has their own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them if you have concerns.

Analytics

GameProphet uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This includes anonymized data such as pages visited, time spent and general location. No personally identifiable information is collected. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via GitHub.
